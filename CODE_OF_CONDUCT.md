# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a place where artists and developers can contribute without
harassment, regardless of experience, identity or language.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and specific when discussing code or art.
- Assume good faith; critique the work, not the person.
- Credit others' work and respect licenses.
- Keep issues and pull requests on topic.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, insults, sexualized comments, doxxing, or publishing private material without
permission. Adult artwork is allowed in head presets only when the author marks it and it
does not involve minors.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions that break these rules, and may ban
repeat offenders. Report problems privately to the maintainer (imboolean) through the
repository's contact channels. Reports are handled confidentially.

This is a small project; common sense and respect go a long way.
